📊 Retail Business Performance Optimization – Power BI Project

🔍 Project Overview

This project simulates a consulting-style retail analytics engagement focused on revenue performance, pricing strategy, and customer retention optimization.

The objective was to transform transactional retail data into actionable business insights using structured data modeling and advanced analytical techniques in Power BI.

🎯 Business Objectives

Evaluate overall business performance (Revenue, Profit, Margin)

Assess discount impact on profitability

Identify revenue leakage risk through RFM-based customer segmentation

Provide strategic recommendations for pricing and retention optimization

🏗️ Data Modeling Approach

Built a Star Schema model:

Fact Table: Transactions

Dimension Tables: Customers, Calendar

Implemented calculated columns for RFM scoring

Created DAX measures for KPI evaluation

Designed dynamic segment-level revenue analysis

📈 Key Insights

Total Revenue: 11.95M with 35% Profit Margin

Average Discount Rate: 15%

No proportional profit gain in higher discount bands

38% of total revenue linked to Lost customers

921 customers identified as At Risk

🧠 Strategic Recommendations

Shift from blanket discounting to targeted promotional strategy

Launch win-back campaigns for high-value lost customers

Proactively engage at-risk customers to reduce churn

Increase upsell initiatives within Champion segment
